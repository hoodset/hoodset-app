# Template selection

Sources reviewed November 27, 2026. The catalog offers editorial starting sets from the supported RH universe. A product relationship establishes why a company appears here; it does not isolate that business in the parent-company token or prove return potential.

Equal initial weights, rounded to 0.01% and totaling 100%. These example weights are not optimized for return or risk.

## AI Compute Hardware

Suppliers of compute or networking hardware used to train and run AI models.

- **NVDA** (33.34% initially): Data-center GPUs and accelerated-computing systems. [Official source](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/).
- **AMD** (33.33% initially): Instinct GPU accelerators for AI and high-performance computing. [Official source](https://www.amd.com/en/products/accelerators/instinct.html).
- **AVGO** (33.33% initially): Custom AI accelerators and data-center networking silicon. [Official source](https://www.broadcom.com/topics/what-is-networking-for-ai).

Three diversified chip businesses, not pure AI revenue exposure. No cloud platforms or application software are included.

## AI Cloud Platforms

Operators of a general-purpose public cloud with managed AI model services.

- **MSFT** (33.34% initially): Azure supplies cloud infrastructure, AI models and agent-building services. [Official source](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/solutions/ai).
- **AMZN** (33.33% initially): AWS supplies cloud compute and Bedrock AI services. The token represents all of Amazon, including retail. [Official source](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/).
- **GOOGL** (33.33% initially): Google Cloud supplies AI infrastructure, models and development services. [Official source](https://cloud.google.com/products/ai).

These tokens represent the entire parent companies. Cloud and AI are only part of their businesses.

## US Equity ETF Mix

Supported Stock Tokens referencing diversified US equity ETFs.

- **SPY** (50% initially): ETF seeking to track the S&P 500 index. [Official source](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/individual/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy).
- **QQQ** (50% initially): ETF tracking the Nasdaq-100 index. [Official source](https://www.invesco.com/qqq-etf/en/about.html).

QQQ tracks large Nasdaq-listed non-financial companies and overlaps SPY. This is not the entire US market or two independent exposures. The underlying ETFs can rebalance their own holdings.

## Limits

Membership and weights were not fixed a year ago. Historical returns apply today’s selected composition retrospectively, exclude dividends and costs, and are not an issued vault track record. A larger trailing return is not a forecast or buy signal. The catalog is not exhaustive; no extra issuer is added merely to make a basket longer.

User edits retain their own weights and membership and appear as custom mixes. Catalog updates cannot change the composition of deployed vaults.
